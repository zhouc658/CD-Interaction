About Me

I’m Crystal Zhou, a junior at The New School studying Design and Technology. I previously studied Studio Art at Stony Brook University, and I enjoy creating both traditional and digital art, including watercolor and Procreate. My recent work explores mental health through interactive and digital media.

As a designer, I enjoy creative coding, visual storytelling, and experimenting with interactive websites to bring ideas to life.
